I'm a Senior Product Manager based in Fortaleza, Brazil, who likes technology a little too much for his own good. My work lives where user needs meet business goals. Right now I'm at **Facio** [uma linha sobre o que a Facio faz], where I own the payments domain.

I'm the kind of PM who reads the docs, opens the terminal, and builds a working prototype instead of writing a ten page spec. I don't live inside a codebase, but I speak the language well enough to hand engineers something they can actually run, and I've put a few of my own things into production.

### What I work on

* **Payments:** my team owns value capture. Whenever money has to come back from a customer, we operate it, no matter which product created the debt. In practice that means three things: money in (accounts and authorization), collections (billing, communication cadence, renegotiation and credit bureau reporting), and closing the loop (bank reconciliation, making sure what the system claims matches what the bank statement says). The dividing line I use: product teams own the policy, payments owns the execution. They decide the terms of the debt, we go get the money.
* **AI:** this is where a lot of my curiosity goes lately. I build functional prototypes to align teams around an idea before we spend engineering time on it, and I've open sourced a set of Prediction Market simulators here on GitHub to explore the mechanics hands on

Before payments, I spent a few years deep in iGaming: loyalty and gamification, multiplayer tournaments, personalized cashback, jackpots, social betting pools, and Brazil only formats like Escadinha.

### A few results I'm proud of

* Built referral and gift campaigns that became some of the core drivers of growth
* Shipped retention and cashback mechanics that reduced churn in a regulated betting market
